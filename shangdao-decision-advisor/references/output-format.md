# Output Format

Use this structure for annual decision recommendations.

## Executive Summary

Provide 3-6 bullets:

- Main market focus.
- Production posture.
- Marketing/sales posture.
- Finance posture.
- Highest-risk assumption.

## Demand Reading

Summarize the demand chart:

- Current year and next year demand stage.
- Regional ranking.
- Total demand trend.
- Private-label trend.
- Any peak or slowdown.

## Historical Diagnosis

Omit for the first year. Otherwise include:

- What previous decisions tried to do.
- What scores and results show.
- What failed or worked.
- Which bottleneck matters most this year.

## Decision Table

Use a table with these columns:

- `Tab`
- `Field`
- `Recommended value`
- `Reason`
- `Confidence`

Confidence values:

- `High`: directly supported by visible data and simple calculation.
- `Medium`: supported by visible data but depends on competitor behavior.
- `Low`: needed but exact value is unavailable from current evidence.

Group rows by tab:

- 年度预算
- 生产运营
- 厂区建设
- 产品配送
- 自由市场
- 网络市场
- 贴牌市场
- 名人签约
- 财务管理
- 股票投资

## Manual Rule Checks

Include a compact table with these columns:

- `Rule area`
- `Check`
- `Result`
- `Action`

Required checks:

- Demand adjustment: whether 沪深300 and competitive-intensity adjustments were applied correctly for the current year.
- Market-share factors: whether each target region has a coherent price, rebate, variety, quality, advertising, retailer, support, delivery, and brand plan.
- Online market: whether expected online sales stay within the 20% regional-share ceiling.
- Private label: whether bid quality, style count, and bid price meet manual requirements.
- Production: whether volume fits normal capacity plus at most 20% overtime.
- Inventory: whether expected carryover and next-year quality penalties are acceptable.
- Capacity: whether expansion/new factory choices obey national capacity restrictions and one-year implementation timing.
- Finance: whether ending cash avoids automatic expensive short-term borrowing and whether share/dividend/buyback decisions obey hard limits.
- Strategic score: which distinct advantage the plan is trying to earn.

## Expected Effects

Estimate:

- Expected sales by channel/region.
- Ending inventory.
- Production utilization.
- Margin/profit direction.
- Cash direction.
- Score categories likely to improve or worsen.

Use ranges when precise calculations are not possible.

## Risks And Watch Items

List only material risks, such as:

- Cash deficit.
- Overproduction.
- Undersupply in fast-growth regions.
- Inventory aging and quality penalty.
- Bond rating decline.
- Interest coverage weakness.
- Celebrity overbid.
- Stock concentration.

## Before Upload Checklist

Provide a concise checklist of fields the user should verify on the site. Do not say the strategy has been uploaded unless the upload action actually happened.
